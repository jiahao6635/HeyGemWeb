# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in HeyGemWeb a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- showing empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and accepting specific, constructive technical feedback;
- focusing disagreement on evidence, trade-offs, and user impact;
- acknowledging mistakes and taking responsibility for their effects;
- prioritizing the health of the overall community over individual preference.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- sustained disruption of discussions or reviews;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual is publicly representing the project. Project spaces include issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments, community events, and other communication channels operated by HeyGemWeb maintainers.

## Reporting and enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior should be reported privately using a contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile at <https://github.com/jiahao6635>. If no private contact method is available, open a neutral issue requesting a private moderation contact without describing sensitive details publicly.

Maintainers will review reports as promptly and fairly as practical, respect the privacy and safety of reporters, and may remove content or restrict participation when necessary. Anyone enforcing this policy must avoid conflicts of interest and disclose them when relevant.

Possible responses include a private correction, warning, temporary restriction, removal from project spaces, or permanent ban, depending on context, severity, and pattern of behavior.
